1. Summary

Card Slop Tycoon is a satirical idle game. We do not have user accounts and we do not operate servers that collect your gameplay data. Your progress is saved only on your device. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress (coins, gems, cards, settings) is stored locally in the app's storage. It is not backed up to the cloud by us and is deleted when you use Settings → Delete my data or uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob and Google may collect and use, as described in Google's partner policy and Privacy Policy: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, app interaction data, and diagnostics/crash information, to serve, measure and secure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions that require it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying "Remove Ads" disables banner and interstitial ads.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (gems, Remove Ads, VIP, themes) are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat (privacy policy) to verify purchases and restore the items you bought. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, and technical data such as your IP address and device/app version, from which it may infer approximate location. We do not receive or store your payment card details. All in-game currency and items are virtual and have no cash value.

5. Age and children

Card Slop Tycoon is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. On first launch the app asks for your birth month and year to apply age-appropriate settings. We store only your age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18+) on your device, never your birth date. If you are under 13 the game does not run, and no advertising or purchase service is started. For players aged 13–17, ads are non-personalised and capped at teen-appropriate content, and the app reminds them to get a parent or guardian's agreement before purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the app, contact games.slopshopai@gmail.com.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data and do not receive personal data from the ad SDK. Data collected by Google through AdMob is controlled by Google under its own policies and retention periods.

7. Security

Network traffic from the app uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local data is kept inside the app's private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit the data we handle to what the game needs.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. We hold no personal data about you; for data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. To contact us about privacy, email games.slopshopai@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above.

10. Not affiliated

Card Slop Tycoon is parody. It uses no generative AI at runtime; all card names and art are pre-written. It is not affiliated with any real trading card game, publisher or AI company.
